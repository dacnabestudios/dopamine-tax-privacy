Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: July 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dopamine Tax, provided by Dacnabe Studios (developer name DAC NA BE Studios), accesses and handles information. It applies to the Dopamine Tax mobile app, package com.dacnabestudios.dopaminetax, and this policy page.

Dopamine Tax does not collect or share user data through the app. Its product data is processed locally on the user's device and is not transmitted to Dacnabe Studios or another company.

1. Information processed on the device

The app uses only the local information needed to provide app protection:

  • onboarding, selected-app, permission, protection, rule, sound, and language state;
  • local access-window, protected-access, and successful-unlock timestamps and counters;
  • an Android package name or an opaque Apple Family Controls selection token; and
  • temporary accelerometer samples while the shake-to-unlock challenge is active.

Configuration and event state remain in app-owned storage. Raw motion samples are used in memory, discarded when the challenge ends, and are not stored or transmitted. Dopamine Tax does not use the microphone, camera, contacts, location, advertising identifiers, or a Dopamine Tax account.

2. Android access and permissions

Android Usage Access can expose broad app-usage events. Dopamine Tax filters those events on the device to the one protected package selected by the user and does not retain unrelated app events. Limited package visibility checks only whether the supported Free apps Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube are installed; the app does not enumerate or upload the device's app inventory.

“Appear on top” lets Android bring the full Dopamine Tax activity forward when an early return is detected. The app does not read screen content, perform gestures, or draw a floating blocker. A foreground service and boot receiver keep the local protection working when Android permits. These permissions can be denied or revoked in Android Settings; protection then appears inactive.

3. iOS access

On iOS, Dopamine Tax uses Apple's Family Controls, Managed Settings, and Device Activity APIs. Apple gives the app an opaque selection token rather than the selected app's identity. That token and the minimum shield/access-window state remain in the app's local App Group storage. Dopamine Tax does not upload them or obtain an installed-app list.

4. Collection, sharing, and third parties

For Google Play's Data safety definition, “collection” means transmitting data off the device. Dopamine Tax transmits no user or product data off-device, so the app declares no data collected and no data shared. It contains no advertising, external analytics, remote crash reporting, account sync, or Dopamine Tax backend.

Apple and Google independently process ordinary platform and store information under their own terms. If the user opens this policy from the app, the operating-system browser requests this public GitHub Pages address. The app adds no identifier, selected-app value, timing, motion data, or other product state to the address. GitHub may process ordinary web-request information under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Dacnabe Studios adds no analytics, advertising, cookies, form, or backend to this page.

5. Retention, security, and deletion

Local configuration remains until the user changes it, clears the app's storage, or uninstalls Dopamine Tax. Stopping protection disables monitoring but intentionally keeps the configuration for later resumption. Unrelated Android usage events and raw motion samples are not retained. Android cloud backup is disabled for the app. Local information is protected by the normal security controls of the user's device and operating system.

To delete all Dopamine Tax app-owned information, clear the app's storage in system Settings or uninstall the app. Because Dacnabe Studios has no server copy or Dopamine Tax account, there is no remote account record to delete, export, or correct.

6. Children

Dopamine Tax is intended for adults aged 18 or older and is not directed to children. Dacnabe Studios does not knowingly collect children's personal data through the app.

7. User choices and privacy rights

Users control the app's permissions in system Settings and may stop protection, change the selected app, clear local storage, or uninstall at any time. For a privacy question or a request under applicable law, contact Dacnabe Studios. Since the app sends no user data to Dacnabe Studios, the developer normally has no app user record to locate or return.

8. Changes and contact

This policy will be updated before Dopamine Tax introduces a practice that changes these disclosures. The effective date above will change when the policy changes.

Privacy contact: Dacnabe Studios — dacnabestudios@gmail.com

“Aparecer sobre outros apps” permite que o Android traga a atividade completa do Dopamine Tax para a frente quando detecta um retorno antecipado. O aplicativo não lê conteúdo da tela, não executa gestos e não desenha um bloqueador flutuante. Um serviço em primeiro plano e um receptor de inicialização mantêm a proteção local quando o Android permite. Esse token e o estado mínimo do escudo e da janela de acesso permanecem no App Group local. O Dopamine Tax não os envia nem obtém uma lista de aplicativos instalados.

O Dopamine Tax não transmite dados do usuário ou do produto, portanto declara que não coleta nem compartilha dados. Apple e Google tratam de forma independente informações normais da plataforma e das lojas, sob seus próprios termos. Se o usuário abrir esta política pelo aplicativo, o navegador do sistema solicita este endereço público do GitHub Pages. O aplicativo não acrescenta identificador, aplicativo selecionado, horário, movimento ou outro estado do produto ao endereço. O GitHub pode tratar informações normais da solicitação web conforme a Declaração Geral de Privacidade do GitHub. A Dacnabe Studios não adiciona analytics, publicidade, cookies, formulário ou backend a esta página.

5. Retenção, segurança e exclusão

A configuração local permanece até que o usuário a altere, limpe os dados do aplicativo ou desinstale o Dopamine Tax. Parar a proteção desativa o monitoramento, mas mantém intencionalmente a configuração para retomada posterior. Eventos de uso de outros aplicativos e amostras brutas de movimento não são retidos. O backup em nuvem do Android está desativado para o aplicativo. As informações locais são protegidas pelos controles normais de segurança do aparelho e do sistema.

Para excluir todas as informações pertencentes ao Dopamine Tax, limpe os dados do aplicativo nas Configurações do sistema ou desinstale-o. Como a Dacnabe Studios não possui cópia em servidor nem conta Dopamine Tax, não existe registro remoto de conta para excluir, exportar ou corrigir.

6. Crianças e adolescentes

O Dopamine Tax é destinado a adultos com 18 anos ou mais e não é direcionado a crianças ou adolescentes. A Dacnabe Studios não coleta conscientemente dados pessoais de crianças pelo aplicativo.
